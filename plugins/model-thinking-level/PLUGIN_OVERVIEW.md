See the selected thinking level at a glance while composing a message, even
on mobile or with a narrow thread pane.

## How it works

BB already includes the thinking level beside the model name and updates it
when you change the selection. This plugin keeps that label visible when
BB's compact layout would normally hide it. Models without a reasoning option
keep their usual display.

## Requirements

Requires BB 0.42 or later. There are no settings, external services, or account
requirements. It works independently of Hide Models. Disabling or removing
it restores BB's normal compact layout.
